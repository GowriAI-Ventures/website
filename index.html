Building toward Super Intelligence

Super Intelligence (SI) is a long-term direction for our venture platform: increasingly capable systems that connect reasoning, knowledge, and action. We build toward that future through focused products in retrieval, agentic workflows, security, and evaluation, with clear measures of usefulness and reliability.

Capabilities

The work spans core AI capabilities from LLM application design and agentic orchestration to retrieval, search, computer vision, and evaluation systems built for real-world product use.

LLM Systems and Agentic Workflows

Designing and shipping production-grade AI systems built on modern language models, tool use, structured outputs, and agentic workflows for complex tasks.

Retrieval, Search, and Knowledge Systems

Building retrieval-augmented workflows with relevant search, vector databases, ranking, and grounded knowledge access so AI products can reason over the right context.

Vision, Evals, and Applied Intelligence

Combining computer vision, document understanding, systematic evaluations, and domain-specific product design to make AI systems more reliable, useful, and measurable.
